Why the W beats a circle
  • It can't be faked. Anyone can buy a round office punch. Nobody has your W die.
  • It's a brand impression ten times over. Every punch stamps your mark into the card, and the customer watches it happen.
  • It audits itself. A regional manager can spot a wrong punch from across a counter — no serial lookup needed.
  • It gives the staff a line. "Let's get you another W." Better than "let me punch that."

05The W die

One custom punch tool per store. This is the piece that makes the program un-fakeable, so it's worth ordering properly.

Standard
Four even strokes, flat cuts top and bottom. Still reads as a W at 8 mm.
Condensed
Narrower footprint — fits more punches in a row if you ever extend past 10 visits.
Angled
Tilted 8°. Hardest to reproduce, and the row of holes looks hand-made rather than machined.
Die spec
  • Size: 8 mm wide × 6.5 mm tall. Big enough to read as a W, small enough that ten of them don't weaken the card.
  • Type: long-reach lever punch, 25 mm throat minimum, so it reaches the middle of a card.
  • Quantity: one per store plus two spares held regionally. Number each tool and log which store holds it.
  • Clearance: keep all artwork 3 mm clear of each slot so a punch never destroys a number.
  • Stock limit: a lever punch handles about 20 pt cleanly. Heavier board needs a tabletop press.
