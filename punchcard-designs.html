AField Guide

BTicket

Oxblood field, gold perforation strip across the top, numbered like a raffle stub. Loudest of the three — it looks like something you won, not something you were handed. Best if the card has to compete for attention at a busy counter, and the deep background hides handling marks.

CPassport

Kraft-toned board, inset keyline border, square stamp boxes instead of circles. Reads as a travel document you collect stamps in — which is exactly the behaviour you want. Pairs best with the rotating monthly ink stamp, and it's the most forgiving of an imperfect hand stamp.

DThe snap-apart combo

One printed piece, perforated. The customer snaps the key tag off and keeps it on their keys; the wallet half holds the punches and the rules. Highest perceived value, and it doubles your odds that at least one half survives to visit 10.

FPrint specs for the vendor

Hand this to Sunny or Seattle Design Lab with your chosen direction. It's already scoped inside Plan 2 items 2 and 3 — add it to those print runs rather than sourcing separately.

SpecValue
Wallet card3.375 × 2.125 in · 1/8 in corner radius · 1/8 in bleed all round
Key tag3.375 × 1.44 in · fully rounded ends · 1/4 in keyring hole, centred 0.28 in from the left edge
Combo4.25 × 2.125 in · vertical perforation at 2.85 in · key tag hole on the stub
Stock18–20 pt C1S for A and B · 18 pt uncoated kraft for C
FinishMatte on the front, uncoated strip on the back where staff write · never gloss, a pen won't take
Colour4/4 CMYK · optional gold foil on the wordmark only
NumberingSequential serial printed on every card. Ask for a start number and a range per store.
Punch clearanceKeep all artwork 3 mm clear of each circle so a punch never destroys a number
First runQuote 2,500 and 10,000 · pilot two stores before the full chain
Before anything prints
  • The six decisions in section 10 of the proposal — stacking, caps and the expiry window all appear in the fine print on the back.
  • The battery at visit 6 — confirm with compliance. If it's out, the back copy changes.
  • The QR square is a placeholder. Drop in the real app-store link before artwork goes out.
  • Serial ranges per store so a card can be traced back to where it was issued.
