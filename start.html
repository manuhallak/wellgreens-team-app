Working from the Mac mini (or any other computer)

  1. Bookmark this page. wellgreens-team.netlify.app/start.html — that one link gets you back to everything, on any machine, with nothing installed.
  2. Open the Team Dashboard and sign in with your wellgreens.store email. That's all you need for day-to-day work. The dashboard, the calendar and the ownership page are all live and shared — whatever you change on one computer shows up on the other.
  3. Only if you want to change how the pages themselves are built, sign in to these three in your browser: GitHub (manuhallak) · Supabase (Wellgreens project) · Netlify (wellgreens-team). See the table below.
  4. Install the Claude desktop app and sign in as manu@wellgreens.store if you want to keep working with Claude there. Your sessions follow your account across desktop, web and mobile. Anything that touches files on a specific computer needs the desktop app open on that computer.
