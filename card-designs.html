Why the W beats a circle
  • It can't be faked. Anyone can buy a round office punch. Nobody has your W die.
  • It's a brand impression ten times over. Every punch stamps your mark into the card while the customer watches.
  • It audits itself. A regional manager spots a wrong punch from across a counter — no serial lookup needed.
  • It gives the staff a line. "Let's get you another W" beats "let me punch that."

05The die

One custom punch per store. This is the piece that makes the whole programme un-fakeable, so it is worth ordering properly.

Standard
Four even strokes, flat cuts top and bottom. Still reads as a W at 8 mm.
Condensed
Narrower footprint if you ever extend the card past ten visits.
Angled
Tilted 8°. Hardest to reproduce, and the finished row looks hand-made.
Die spec
  • Size — 8 mm wide × 6.5 mm tall. Large enough to read as a W, small enough that ten of them don't weaken the card.
  • Type — long-reach lever punch, 25 mm throat minimum, so it reaches the middle of the card.
  • Quantity — one per store plus two spares held regionally. Number each tool and log which store holds it.
  • Clearance — all artwork stays 3 mm clear of every slot so a punch never destroys a number.
  • Stock limit — a lever punch handles about 20 pt cleanly. Heavier board needs a tabletop press.
